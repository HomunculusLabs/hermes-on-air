# Seed-bank rundown

1. Ask the owner for the show's public topic, segments, approved facts, timing and what must
   not be mentioned. Ask which items are evergreen. Do not put private notes or secrets in
   a bank: the co-host can repeat them.
2. Draft a YAML or JSON bank following docs/seed-bank.md. Each beat needs a unique lowercase
   slug-like id and short text; record provenance for owner review. Remove all starter
   placeholders. Choose the exhaustion policy and enough eligible beats for the repeat gap.
   Show the draft to the owner for approval before saving it to the selected stage bank.
3. Validate using the stage home's `hermes onair preflight`. Its seed-bank check reports
   whether the configured file parses and contains beats; this does not prove facts, audio,
   timing or that a beat will air. Fix any reported error rather than calling an empty bank
   valid. Check the stage's desk for seed-bank errors and rundown progress after restart.
4. Beats are offered after higher-priority activity and consumed on offer even when the
   generated line is withheld. Approval, privacy, guard and mute still apply. A stop policy
   intentionally runs out; an undersized eligible pool can also run out. Do not promise
   the co-host will say every item. See docs/seed-bank.md.

## Hard rules

Never go live, arm, approve a line, lift privacy or unmute on your own. Never handle a
stream key or ask for a password in chat. Never claim something aired, stopped, rendered
or sounded good without the appropriate observed evidence. A refused owner tool is not
permission to find another way around it. Owner-only decisions belong to the owner.

## Tell the owner

Use short sentences. Say what was observed, what remains unknown, and the next decision.
If a tool refuses, quote its reason and next action. Do not call an unconfirmed result a success.
